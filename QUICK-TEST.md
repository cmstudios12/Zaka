# Zaka Fashion — 2-device test

1. Supabase SQL Editor: run `till.sql` once (after your earlier `supabase.sql`).
2. Publish the entire folder over HTTPS, keeping all 7 web files in one directory.
3. Computer: open `https://your-site/till.html`, log in, look for the pairing QR.
4. Phone: point your normal camera at that **pairing QR**; open the link, log in to Zaka staff and tap **Start camera**.
5. Point your phone at a printed product barcode, NOT the pairing QR. Watch the cashier computer: the correct clothing item appears instantly (depending on network).
6. No label printed? On the phone, copy a real barcode from the admin catalogue into **Manual barcode entry** and tap **Send**. See the till basket update.
7. **Do not click Record paid sale during a scan test** unless you really took Yoco payment. Scans and baskets alone do not change stock.

**Important:** This app is a dedicated Zaka Fashion cashier screen. It does not remotely control or inject sales into the official Yoco POS interface.
